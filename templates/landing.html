How It Works

A concise four-step workflow from paper bill to verified corporate reimbursement.

01

Upload a Receipt

Photograph an itemized bill or upload an image/PDF document along with digital payment proof (UPI or Card).

02

Review Extracted Info

Inspect detected merchant, date, and amount. Accept the extracted values or make manual corrections before submitting.

03

Validate Policy & Caps

FINANCIAL GOVERNANCE & SECURITY

Trust, Access Control & Data Protection

Actual implemented security mechanisms safeguarding employee privacy and organizational funds.

🔐

Backend-Enforced RBAC

Strict server-side role separation. Employees can never view another employee's private receipts, approve their own claims, or elevate their privileges.

🛡️

Administrator Approval Gate

Public registration does not grant dashboard access. All new staff accounts remain in PENDING_APPROVAL status until verified by an Administrator.

📁

Cryptographic Integrity

Every receipt is hashed with SHA-256 for bitwise tamper detection and 64-bit difference hashing (dHash) to flag cropped or re-photographed bills.

📜

Immutable Audit History

Every state change, document request, resubmission, and approval decision records the actor, timestamp, and rationale in an audit-ready log.
