CORE CAPABILITIES

Engineered for Complete Expense Integrity

Six implemented capabilities designed for corporate policy enforcement, automated auditing, and finance team productivity.

📷

1. Smart Receipt Scanning

Upload or photograph an itemized paper receipt. The multi-stage pipeline applies skew correction, contrast enhancement, and neural OCR to extract merchant, date, amount, currency, and line items.

⚖️

2. Policy Validation

Evaluate every expense claim against structured company policy rules, destination city meal per-diems, home office local caps, and project reimbursement thresholds using decimal-accurate arithmetic.

🛡️

3. Duplicate Detection

Identify potential duplicate receipts and transactions through exact SHA-256 byte hashes, 64-bit perceptual difference hashes (dHash), recycled UPI references, and cross-employee collusion checks.

⚡

4. Approval Workflow

Route compliant claims at or below per-person project caps to automatic approval. Claims exceeding caps or triggering risk flags automatically route to Accounts personnel for detailed review.

📊

5. Expense Tracking

Track explicit claim lifecycles from draft, submission, Accounts review, and document requests, through approval and actual reimbursement with full status visibility and in-app notifications.

📜

6. Auditability

Preserve immutable claim history, receipt versions, original uploaded documents, reviewer notes, and human field corrections with complete actor, timestamp, and rationale attribution.

TRANSPARENT PROCESS

How It Works

A concise four-step workflow from paper bill to verified corporate reimbursement.

01

1. Upload a Receipt

Photograph an itemized bill or upload an image/PDF document along with digital payment proof (UPI or Card).

02

2. Review Extracted Information

Inspect detected merchant, date, and amount. Accept the extracted values or make manual corrections before submitting.

03

3. Validate Policy & Caps

The backend verifies project reimbursement caps, home office meal limits, non-reimbursables, and duplicate flags.

04

4. Track & Reimburse

Compliant claims auto-approve; exceptions route to Accounts review. Follow live progress through final payment disbursement.

SECURITY & GOVERNANCE

Trust, Access Control & Data Protection

Implemented security mechanisms safeguarding corporate funds and preserving financial integrity.

🔐

Backend-Enforced RBAC

Strict server-side role separation. Employees cannot access private receipts of other staff, approve their own claims, or elevate privileges.

🛡️

Administrator Approval Gate

Public registration creates pending accounts. No employee or accounts user can access protected dashboards until reviewed by an authorized Admin.

📁

Cryptographic Document Integrity

Every receipt is hashed with SHA-256 for exact bitwise verification and 64-bit difference hashing (dHash) to detect cropped or re-photographed bills.

📜

Immutable Audit History

Every claim lifecycle event, reviewer action, document request, and resubmission records the actor, timestamp, and rationale in a versioned audit trail.
